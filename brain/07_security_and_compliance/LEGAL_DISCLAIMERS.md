# Legal Disclaimers & Policy Text

> These texts are drafts for an academic prototype. Have them reviewed by qualified counsel before any real-world deployment.

## 1. Decision-support disclaimer (shown at first login, in every dossier, in the Copilot footer)
ExportReady is an **informational decision-support tool**. It is **not** legal, regulatory or consulting advice, **not** a
certification, audit or inspection, and **does not guarantee** FDA clearance, Green List admission or release of any
shipment at the US border. Results depend entirely on the information and evidence you provide and on the rule base as of
the displayed rule versions, which may lag official changes. Regulatory decisions rest with FDA and the competent
authorities. Consult qualified regulatory professionals before shipping.

## 2. Accuracy of rule base
Rules summarise public sources (21 CFR, FDA Import Alert 76-01, QMSR/ISO 13485:2016 references, ASTM F899/A967 citations).
Citations are pointers; the official text governs. Effort-day figures are planning estimates, not commitments.

## 3. AI-assisted features
OCR extraction, buyer-condition matching and the Copilot may be wrong or incomplete. They never change a verdict on their own;
you must review and confirm their output. The Copilot answers only from the sources it cites and may decline to answer.

## 4. Third-party content
Names of standards bodies and registrars are for reference; no endorsement, affiliation or ranking is implied. ASTM/ISO standards
are copyrighted by their owners; ExportReady does not reproduce them — obtain them from the publishers.

## 5. Privacy (summary)
Data collected: account email, factory profile, orders, evidence files and metadata. Purpose: provide the service. Isolation per
tenant; no sale of data; no cross-tenant sharing; AI calls process only data needed for the request. Users may request export or
deletion of their data. (Full policy text to be finalised with institutional guidance.)

## 6. Limitation of liability
The prototype is provided "as is" for educational use. The authors and UMT Sialkot are not liable for losses arising from
reliance on its output.

## 7. Where displayed
First-login acceptance gate; footer on every page; PDF dossier cover and footer; Copilot answers; Gatekeeper results.
