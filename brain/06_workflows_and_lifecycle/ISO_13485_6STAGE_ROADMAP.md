# ISO 13485 — 6-Stage Roadmap & Registrar Directory (F12)

Status: Specified, content static. Context: QMSR (effective 2 Feb 2026) incorporates ISO 13485:2016, so an ISO 13485
QMS is the backbone of FDA readiness. **ISO 13485 certification is not itself FDA clearance or Green List admission.**

## The six stages

| Stage | Name | Typical outputs | Related rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Gap analysis & scoping** | Gap report vs ISO 13485:2016 and 21 CFR 820; scope, product families, exclusions | all (baseline = ExportReady findings) |
| 2 | **QMS design & documentation** | Quality manual, procedures, DMR/Medical Device File, document control | ER-DOC-013/015/016 |
| 3 | **Implementation & training** | Operating processes, validated passivation/cleaning, calibration system, UDI labelling, training records | ER-MAT, ER-PAS, ER-CAL, ER-UDI, ER-CLN |
| 4 | **Internal audit & management review** | Audit reports, CAPA, management review minutes | ER-QMS-004, ER-CAPA-025, ER-DOC-017 |
| 5 | **Certification audit** | Registrar Stage 1 (documentation) + Stage 2 (implementation) audits, nonconformity closure, certificate | evidence tier 1 |
| 6 | **Surveillance & FDA maintenance** | Annual surveillance audits, recertification, FDA registration renewal, Green List upkeep | ER-REG-001/002 |

The app maps each stage to the open GAP/BLOCKER rules of the factory and shows progress (% rules MET among the stage's rules).
